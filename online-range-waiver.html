SIGN YOUR WAIVER ONLINE!

At Lake Erie Arms, we value your time and want to make your visit as smooth as possible. By signing our range waiver online before your visit, you can reduce your wait time at the store and spend more time doing what you love – shooting!

Here's how it works:

  1. Click the button to log in or create an account.
  2. Fill out the required information.
  3. Review, sign and submit your waiver.
  4. When you arrive at our facility, just let our staff know you've already signed your waiver online.
