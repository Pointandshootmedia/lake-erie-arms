Layaway Policy

At Lake Erie Arms, we understand the importance of responsible firearm ownership. To make it easier for you to acquire the firearm of your choice, we proudly offer a convenient layaway option.

How it Works

  1. Choose Your Firearm: Select the firearm you've been eyeing from our extensive collection.
  2. Down Payment: Make an initial 20% down payment to secure your chosen firearm. This down payment ensures that your firearm will be reserved exclusively for you.
  3. Flexible Payment Plan: Enjoy the flexibility of paying for your firearm over time. We offer customizable payment plans to suit your budget.
  4. Pick Up Your Firearm: Once your payments are complete, come into our store to pick up your firearm. Our team will guide you through the necessary paperwork to ensure a smooth and legal transaction.

Benefits of Layaway

  • Budget-Friendly: Spread the cost of your firearm purchase over several weeks or months, making it more manageable for your budget.
  • Secure Your Choice: Layaway ensures that the firearm you want is reserved exclusively for you, avoiding the risk of it being sold to someone else.
  • No Interest Charges: Unlike traditional financing options, our layaway program doesn't come with interest charges, offering you a cost-effective way to make your purchase.

Important Information

  • Layaway is available for in-store purchases only.
  • A 20% minimum down payment is required to initiate the layaway process.
  • Layaway must be paid in full within 90 days. Failure to pay in full or cancellation of layaway will result in the forfeit of your initial 20% deposit.
  • No layaways on transactions under $249.
  • No more than three layaways at a time.
  • Not all items are eligible for the Layaway Plan. Some of the ineligible items are all NFA regulated items, high demand items, certain "Sale" items, and certain high-dollar firearms and all consignment firearms.
  • Layaway is subject to our store's terms and conditions and the manager reserves the right to alter terms of the agreement.

Ready to reserve your firearm? Visit Lake Erie Arms today and take advantage of our layaway program to make your firearm ownership dreams a reality!
