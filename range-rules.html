range rules

Basic Safety

Safety is of first importance at the range, not just for yourself but for the other people at the range. Follow the basic rules of gun safety.

  1. ALWAYS Keep The Gun Pointed In A Safe Direction.
  2. ALWAYS Keep Your Finger Off The Trigger Until Ready To Shoot.
  3. ALWAYS Keep The Gun Unloaded Until Ready To Use.

Range Courtesy

In addition to basic safety, there are a few range-related guidelines designed to enhance the safety and experience of everyone at the range.

  • Each range visit limited to :50 minutes unless there is no wait.
  • ALL LOANER GUNS MUST USE LAKE ERIE ARMS AMMO.
  • NO TRACER ROUNDS, STEEL CORE, INCENDIARY GAS, EXPLOSIVES, BUCKSHOT and NO ARMOR PIERCING ROUNDS.
  • Customer ammo subject to inspection by Lake Erie Arms staff.

Range Restrictions

  • AGES 10–17 ADULT SUPERVISION REQUIRED
  • AGES 18+ NO RESTRICTIONS. Minimum age to purchase handgun ammunition is 21 and handgun rental is 21.
