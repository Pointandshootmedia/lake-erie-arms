UNLEASH YOUR POTENTIAL

Welcome to the LEA Training Academy, where safety, skill, and confidence come together. Our academy is dedicated to providing comprehensive and hands-on firearms training for individuals of all experience levels. All classes are taught by LEA Training Academy staff certified by the NRA, USCCA, and/or Ohio Peace Officer Training Academy and offer decades of experience in military, healthcare, law enforcement, and firearms instruction.

Whether you're a complete beginner looking to learn the fundamentals of safe firearm handling or an experienced shooter seeking to sharpen your skills, our expert instructors are here to guide you on your journey. We believe that responsible firearm ownership starts with education, and our academy is designed to empower you with the knowledge and expertise you need to make informed decisions and stay safe. Join us in a supportive and engaging learning environment, and let's build a safer, more responsible community together.
