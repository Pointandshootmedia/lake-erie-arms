EXPERIENCE THE LEA DIFFERENCE

At Lake Erie Arms, we're dedicated to offering meticulous and comprehensive gunsmithing services while giving you the chance to make them truly one-of-a-kind to meet the needs of every shooter, from casual enthusiasts to seasoned professionals. Our skilled on-site gunsmith brings years of experience and precision to every task.

Whether you're looking to fine-tune your trigger for a smoother pull, require detailed custom machining, or want to add a personal touch with our custom cerakote finishes and laser engraving, we have the expertise to deliver top-quality results. Our team is here to help you create a firearm that's not only reliable, but reflects your unique style. Explore our range of services to enhance the performance, aesthetics, and functionality of your firearms.

Meet Your Gunsmith

Andy “Gundalf” Lake

Andy has been in the firearm industry as a Gunsmith for over 10 years, but he's been passionate about firearms for his entire life. Andy, an Army Veteran of 6 years in the Ohio Army National Guard, operated as a Gunsmith in both firearm shops and his own small custom shop, working on handguns and long guns of all kinds. He strives for excellence in his craft, focusing on superior service to the customer and ensuring quality of service over all else.

Andy is not only a Gunsmith, but also a Certified Firearms Instructor through the NRA and USCCA, teaches LEA's AR-15 Build Classes, and is also a volunteer advisor for his local 4H shooting sports program, helping to educate the next generation of firearms enthusiasts.
