TOPSHOT CLAY HOUSE

INDOOR CLAY SHOOTING · $25/GAME

Our TopShot Clay House (trademark pending) offers a one-of-a-kind experience that you won't find anywhere else! Much like Topgolf, the TopShot Clay House offers game modes like Trap, Skeet, Sporting Clays, and 5-Stand. Each of the 8 semi-private shooting bays accommodates up to 4 shooters per suite so that you can play and shoot with friends and family.

Ammo used in the TopShot Clay House must be purchased on-site.
